ten places · no pay · a name in the credits

beta testers wanted.

the doors are open · the kitchen is not finished

i have been building a place for a while now. it is a cafe at the end of the web: eleven regulars, a terminal that asks your name, a jukebox, a garden, a zine, and a door at the back that stays shut until the last day. it started as a webcomic that never got finished and somewhere along the way it became somewhere you can walk around in.

it still has rough edges, and i would rather find them with you than alone. so i am looking for ten people to come in early. you play, you poke at things, and you tell me what needs attention — a broken button, a dead end, a room that does not feel finished, a joke that does not land.

dm @cafedreamr the word beta open the application form

or comment beta on the post and i will come to you. the screening is exactly the five questions below — nothing is hidden, and there is no interview unless you would like one; the form only also asks where to reach you.

01 · the trade

what you give, what you get

what i am asking for

  • about an hour a week for a few weeks — honestly, not generously
  • whatever device you actually use: a phone is the most useful, then a laptop
  • that you write down what you find. one line, or one screenshot, per thing
  • that you tell me if something is boring. boredom is a bug here

what you get

  • the doors, early — everything i have finished, and the parts i have not
  • a name in the credits, if you want one, spelled how you like
  • that whatever you report gets fixed, and you will be told when it is
  • no pay, no contract, no deadline, and no obligation to stay

there is no build to install. it runs in a browser, from a link i send you, and it is being changed most days — so if a thing is broken at noon it may be gone by dinner.

02 · the screen

the five questions

five, then i tell you yes or no-thank-you honestly. copy them, answer them however you like — a dm is fine, a voice note is fine, a numbered wall is fine.

  1. what should i call you, and roughly where are you? a timezone is plenty. i am not keeping a dossier, i just want to know when you are awake.
  2. what will you play it on — phone, laptop, or both, and which browser? almost every bug in this project so far has been a layout bug on a phone. this question finds them.
  3. what kind of games and worlds do you like? one or two names is plenty. so i can guess what you will notice. it is not a taste test.
  4. how much time can you honestly give it in a week — twenty minutes, an hour, more? twenty honest minutes beats an hour of good intentions.
  5. will you write down what you find and send it to me? one line per thing is enough. silent testers are the one thing i cannot use.

and: would you rather i email you, or stay here in the dm? either is fine. if you are unsure about any of it, say so — that is allowed.

04 · the line

what i will not ask for

not collected, not wanted

  • your real name, your address, your age, your phone number, or anything like them
  • a scan of anything, an account password, or a copy of an id
  • anything you are not happy for a stranger to keep in an inbox

what you send stays between us. nothing unreleased goes public before the launch, credited or not, and you can stop whenever you like — say the word and i will take your name off everything and delete what you sent. one rule, and it is the only one: be kind in the dm.
