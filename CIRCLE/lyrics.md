## 00:00 · MIKI — "Numbered"
*Luke 12:7 — "Indeed, the very hairs of your head are all numbered. Don't be afraid; you are worth more than many sparrows."*

Midnight and the screen glows green,
lines of code I've never seen,
the scientists are counting sheep,
new specimens asleep, asleep.

They collect, they file, they score,
data stacked from floor to floor.
One arm of steel, one heart of skin.
They count the outside, never in.

My face is mine, my circuits hum.
The twins make music — here I come.
Yellow, black and chrome inspire.
Glitch sounds like home, like going home.

But every hair upon my head is numbered,
every tear I cried is numbered,
not by them with all their charts,
by the one who holds our hearts.
He found me at my very worst,
empty hands and backwards-cursed,
took me in and called me loved.
Data can't do what he does.

## 06:00 · STAR — "The Squirrel Hour"
*Matthew 6:27; 11:28 — "Who of you by worrying can add a single hour to your life? … Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest."*

Six o'clock and the light comes thin
through the window where I watch them spin,
little thieves on the garden wall,
stealing minutes, that's all, that's all.

Who by worrying can add one hour?
Not me, not the squirrel, not the flower,
so I pour the morning slow and deep
and give the stolen hours to sleep.

Caffusion, lavender and steam,
mint for the waking, cardamom for the dream.
Come to me, you weary, rest your head.
The chair is taken — take the window instead.

Misha curls where I meant to sit,
lavender fluff and attitude to fit,
every morning a new devise
to coax a bengal out of paradise.

Bible open and the pages breathe,
come to me is all I need.
The squirrels keep what they take from me,
and I keep the sunrise, quiet and free.

## 09:00 · CHARI — "First Lessons"

Mochi, Crumb, the morning's new.

## 12:00 · MILO — "The Still Hour"
*Luke 17:21; John 15:4 — "The kingdom of God is within you. … Dwell in me, and I in you."*

Midday and the garden holds its breath,
mint and sage and nothing left
to prove, to hurry, to defend.
Be still and know, and just attend.

Hands in soil and the soil is kind.
The kingdom isn't hard to find,
not in the temple, not afar.
It's where you are, it's where you are.

Dwell in me and I in you.
The branch holds fast, the root holds true.
The kingdom of God is within.
Breathe it out and breathe it in.

Come and sit, I'll teach you how.
The breath you have is here and now,
in like the evening rain,
out with the worry, out with the pain.

Stay a while, the tea can wait.
The garden never comes in late.

## 15:00 · HANS — "The Outside of the Cup"
*Luke 11:39 — "Now then, you clean the outside of the cup and dish, but inside you are full of greed and wickedness."*

My fathers poured for emperors and kings,
precise as clockwork, hollow things,
the whisk was right, the bow was low,
but no one asked what hearts should know.

I have been many men, I confess,
the scholar, the fop, the bloodied guest.
I walked the earth, I slipped the veil,
till love laid for me a stronger trail.

I lived between the worlds, you see,
neither the night nor noon owned me,
then I was caught at last on solid ground,
and in the catching, I was found.

You clean the outside of the cup,
but lift it, drink, and wake up.
The rite is nothing if the heart's untrue.
Pour it out and start anew.

Now I teach the pour, the seat, the phrase.
Manners are just love with grace.
Earl grey, no blood, the past forgiven.
Etiquette — the small door into heaven.
