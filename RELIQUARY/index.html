the mission
all things are possible · by xen

CafeDreamr exists to show anyone who is interested one thing: all things are possible — especially when we believe that God is helping us in everything.

Even every thought can — and does — become a thing, depending on how much we believe that it is possible.

Take the word apart with me. Be, meaning exist. And lieve — not how I hear it, but how I perceive it: live.

BE — LIEVEexist · live as if it is possible

When we act as if something is possible — when we live it — the thing we thought was impossible becomes possible with God.

"For with God nothing shall be impossible."

Luke 1:37

My lore is just a creative way of expressing that. Chimera cats who escaped an experiment. A portal into other realms. Every story in this realm is the same teaching wearing a costume: what you dare to believe, you may begin to live.

believe one thing possible

Write down one impossible thing, and believe it into a thing.

nothing here yet — give me one impossible thing.

So let us begin. Go and explore — every door in this anthology is interactive, and we shall see you on the inside.

— Xen
