editor's note

so this is issue two. issue one came back from the photocopier smelling like burnt toner and hans filed a formal complaint about “archival integrity.” fine. this one's better anyway: a brand-new realm, a committee of rats running the interior design, a lookbook that actually slaps, and a sticker with your name on it — figuratively.

it's yours if you want it. that's the whole point of a zine.

— the rat

the feature

CHARI'S NEW REALM

or: how to furnish a home with two rats and a hunch

chari — youngest of the three kittens, star's little sister, apprentice to literally everyone — finally got a realm of her own. it's a garden-home: a glass roof that lets the stars in, a warm brick oven, trailing plants in every corner, and a moodboard wall of pinned fabric swatches and torn-out magazine pages. it smells like rain on warm stone.

she didn't plan it alone. nobody plans anything alone around here. she planned it with a committee of two: crumb and mochi, her white rats — the same small companions who ride her shoulders through the loud days and keep her steady when the lab-memories get close. turns out they're also excellent interior designers.

if a rat naps on it, it stays.

the rat method

  1. lay everything on the floor. every cushion, every swatch, every lamp. the floor is the moodboard, and the moodboard is the floor.
  2. release the committee. crumb goes first — crumb is bold, crumb has opinions. mochi follows — mochi is the inspector, and mochi does not care about your feelings.
  3. the nap test. whichever swatch gets slept on, stays. a doze is the highest rating we have. two rats asleep at once is a landmark decision.
  4. the crumb test. if crumb leaves crumbs on it, it belongs in the kitchen. if it comes out clean, it's furniture you're allowed to love.
  5. the tiebreak. if they disagree, it waits by the window until they don't. daylight is a fair referee and a better designer than most of us.

the garden & the pets

the garden is half borrowed from milo's rows — a raised bed of mint the rats are not supposed to raid (they raid it), a herb wall she labels in her own handwriting, and a small courtyard where crumb and mochi patrol like two tiny wardens. the pets list reads: crumb (bold), mochi (inspector), and a jar of tadpoles that hans insists, at length, are “not pets.”

that's the whole trick of it: the realm is calm because it was decided by two animals who only ever want somewhere warm to sleep. chari says it's the first room she's had that doesn't feel like it's waiting for something bad. we could all use a committee like that.
