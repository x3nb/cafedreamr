i. beginning

come in quietly.

this is plain on purpose. white wall, black ink. no noise. for whoever traveled far and through pain.

Sit down. Breathe. If you want company, tap the little scroll on the right edge — letters will slide out.

ii. whispers

verses about how you are loved.

touch one. it will shiver, then glow. plain promise: you are seen, not condemned, called by name.

iii. the tiny library

eight small books.

miniature spines in purple, black and grey. curses broken, homes blessed, family covered, walking with Yeshua. tap a spine to open it.

dust, quiet. pick one.
