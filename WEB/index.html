caught in it

charlotte · cc-006 · white spider · she speaks

This is not so much a room as the thing holding the rooms together. Every question anyone has ever asked in this realm is archived in here, every photograph kept, everything anybody said still findable — and a white spider called Charlotte rests in the middle of all of it, and has for as long as anyone can remember.

She is not a hybrid. She is a spider, whole and speaking, and the least hurried person you will ever meet. She rests almost all the time. She wakes for exactly two things: a bug, or a song. Whatever is caught, she eats it or she listens to it — and then it is mending: whatever the others tore today, stitched back up, plus the realms stitched to each other, plus Miki's mail, read at Miki's request.

01 · the cabinet

the drawers that only open if you answer

six drawers · five questions · one that was never locked at all

There is a chest of drawers against the wall of the web, and it is not locked with keys. Each drawer has a question set into the front of it, and the drawer will not slide until the question is answered. Charlotte finds this entirely reasonable. She says a drawer you have to think about is a drawer you remember.

Answer one and it comes out on its own, with a sound like a small door giving up. Answer badly and it only waits — she keeps no score and there is no penalty here, and after two tries she starts to help. She insists that the helping is not her being kind. It is her being efficient.

the questions are not there to keep you out. they are there so that when the drawer opens, you are paying attention.

— charlotte

One of the six was never locked. It was waiting for something to be put in it, which is a different kind of drawer altogether. Whatever you leave in there stays in there, on this device, for you.

02 · the shelf

things fall off the shelf

for no reason at all · which is the entire point

The shelf in the corner holds a spool of silk, a cup, a thimble, a pebble, a marble, a spoon, and one moth. Every so often — with no warning, no cause, and no pattern anybody has ever been able to find — one of them goes over the edge. It falls. It bounces once. It rolls. It stops.

Nothing is ever broken. She has never once been upset about it, and she does not consider it a problem to be solved. She comes down on a thread, hooks the thing, and lifts it back where it was. If you would rather do it yourself, click whatever has fallen and she will hand you the thread.

the falling is not a fault. the shelf is high and things are round, and that is the whole of the explanation.

— charlotte
she is watching, at her own pace

03 · caught in the web

the songs she has caught

she wakes for a bug or a song · she keeps the songs

A song gets caught the same way a bug does: suddenly, and stuck. When one does, she stops resting, walks out along the strand to wherever it is tangled, and listens to the whole thing before she lets it go. Some of them she keeps. The ones she has kept so far are pinned to the strands below — every one of them came out of a drawer, so they are yours to replay for as long as you like.

Her complete collection lives in her music web, where every node is a song and the whole thing is strung the way she strings everything: carefully, and with room to move.

04 · the door at the back

the game room

sealed · kept for the last day · not a puzzle you win today

There is a door at the back of the web that Charlotte is holding shut, and she is entirely serious about it. Behind it is the game room: the loudest room in the whole realm, all drums and brass and a timer running down, with a boss at the end of it and every challenge anybody has ever thought of, saved up.

It is not for now. It is for the last day — after everything else is finished and there is nothing left to do but win. She wants it to still be exciting when you finally get there, and she is right about that. So the door stays shut.

She has not decided what kind of game it is yet. She says that is the fun part, and that she will know when it is time.

It is not silent, though. Put your ear against it and you can hear the thing practising.

not yet. that one is for the end — the big one, everything on fire, the last boss. i am keeping it warm in here until you have finished the rest. come back when there is nothing left but the door.

— charlotte
