Unchained

hans · the stacks · CC-005 — the realm's historian, keeper of the library and the academy, and its anchor of etiquette. it is his own story, and he tells it in his own voice.

hans tells it the way he tells everything — with the kettle on and no hurry about it. the room with no windows. the girl with the fox ears, who was there before any of us. the girl in the next cage, whose door was yellow. the night the lock finally gave. the light that came down through the ceiling, and everyone who was left looking for it afterwards. he has only ever told it here.

his voice, this telling: a man in his twenties — english, softly received-pronunciation, faintly posh. a tea-snob, a little languid — charming, eloquent, and in no hurry whatsoever. gentle, soft-spoken, patient above all, as though he has all the time in the world and fully intends to use it. spoken, never sung. under it, very low and even: a slow tap, like a fingertip on taut thread, marking the pauses.
