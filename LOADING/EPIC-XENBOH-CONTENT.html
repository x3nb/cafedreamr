ABOUT

Artist · Developer · 80s Enthusiast

Raised on neon lights and synth lines, Alex Mercer blends code and canvas to deconstruct nostalgia into contemporary works. Trained in fine arts and self-taught in web technologies, Alex prototypes interactive installations that feel like found fragments from a parallel 1984—bold silhouettes, saturated palettes, and tactile mischief.

When not building generative visuals or responsive micro-sites, Alex explores vintage fashion archives and rewrites garment patterns into shader code, marrying texture and algorithm.

ART

Exhibitions · Series · Mediums

Signature series include "Neon Collation" — mixed-media panels with embedded LED matrices — and "Glitch Couture", a digital clothing line rendered as browser-based wearables. Visual language references 80s magazine layouts, VHS artifacts, and runway photography with high-contrast color blocking.

Mediums: oil on panel, generative canvas (WebGL), wearable electronics, and limited-run zines.

PORTFOLIO

Selected Projects · Case Studies

Highlights: a responsive portfolio site that emulates a 1980s magazine flipbook; a realtime audio-visualizer for live sets; and a boutique e-comm theme inspired by vintage department-store catalogs. Each project balances craftsmanship, accessibility, and performative color systems.

Tech stack: HTML/CSS, JavaScript, WebGL (Three.js), GLSL shaders, Node.js, and small-form microcontrollers for interactive garments.
