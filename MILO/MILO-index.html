realm 04 · paradise garden · milo keeps it

leave it at the gate.

two lamps · a prayer · an inspiration

the garden is the part of the realm that faces the dark. milo is out here most nights — in the hammock strung between the rows, or down on his knees in the mint with his sleeves pushed up, doing the thing he never explains. he has never once asked anybody what they were carrying. he just moves the watering can and makes room on the bench.

so there are two lamps at the gate now. at one you can leave a prayer: whatever you are carrying, named plainly, because naming it is most of the work. at the other you can leave an inspiration: something small and true that helped you, that somebody else might need at two in the morning. both are left the same way, and both go up the same way.

04 · the etiquette

what belongs here

the only four rules the garden has

  • a prayer is a plain sentence, addressed however you like. there is no correct way to do this, and no hurry in it either.
  • an inspiration is something that actually worked. small is not a failure — small is usually the whole thing.
  • no names of other people, no addresses, no screenshots of anybody's private life. the sky is anonymous by default and it should stay that way.
  • if something is unkind, it comes down. the garden keeps itself quiet, and milo reads everything before it rises.

if tonight is heavier than a page can help with, the café has a whole room for that — support · and the door is never locked. you do not have to explain yourself to leave something here. you do not have to come back and check on it either. it will go up on its own.
