System Lore

In the year 1889, the coal grids failed. Society didn't collapse; it simply shifted to a high-pressure, highly caffeinated alternative.

By boiling the mysterious, hyper-reactive Aether-Pekoe leaf inside massive subterranean pressure engines, whole sectors are now powered entirely by aromatic tea-steam.

The pressure is high, the porcelain is steel-reinforced, and missing tea-time is considered a grand act of treason against the Steam-Crown.

Aether Blends

Clockwork Chamomile: A glowing, lavender-tinted pressure brew layered with micro-flecked copper dust. Engineered to soothe rogue mechanical joints and human nerves simultaneously.

Voltaic Earl Grey: Harvested during high-altitude lightning storms in the floating gardens of Neo-Assam. It gives off a small static charge when stirred with a silver spoon.

Sub-Zero Matcha: Cryogenically crushed using liquid nitrogen valves. Used by long-distance zeppelin crews to keep frostbite away from their steering hands.
