• Visual Strategy: A floorplan-inspired graphic showing a strict divider wall between two zones.
• Headline Text: Health Codes & Zoning Laws
• Content Points:
	• Dual Zoning: Your property must be zoned for both food service and animal boarding.
	• Air Separation: You must install separate HVAC/air-ventilation systems so dander never reaches the kitchen.
	• The Physical Barrier: Floor-to-ceiling walls must separate where food is prepared from where animals live.

• Headline Text: Essential Space Design
• Content Points:
	• 🚪 The Double-Door Airlock: A two-door entryway system into the cat lounge. This prevents animals from making a run for the front door.
	• 🏥 The Isolation Suite: A completely separate, private quarantine area for new arrivals or sick animals.

• Visual Strategy: A checklist style with checkmark icons.
