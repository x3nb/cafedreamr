who she is

Half cat, half machine, all kindness under the mischief. Made in a laboratory like the rest of them, saved from it, and quick to say sorry the moment she oversteps — because she remembers what it was like to be the joke, and she keeps her own jokes light on purpose.

She owns the zine (issue 01: made of mischief & toner), the newsletter (mikimail: the email that runs on absolution and a dream), and a standing agreement with the light: it stays on, she supervises.
