The claw machine

The claw is very slow and very sincere. It has never once let go of anything on purpose, but it has also never once picked anything up on purpose, so it comes out even — which is the most you can ask of a machine.

one token a go, and the tokens come out of the other machines — go wind the box, it pays. the claw has a manifest taped to the back of the glass and it does not always honour the manifest. and there is a corner of paper under the marbles. it’s nothing. it has been nothing for a while now. — miki, on a card in the coin return
