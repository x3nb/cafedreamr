Bobby built it, and bobby put up the sign, and he says both were his idea and both are staying. It is a box with a lid, a cup, four trays, a delay, one thing you can smell through the tape, and a camera — every part bolted on to answer a complaint the last test left. This machine is the other half: the walking. Take the finished box down the path, put it on the mat, and be gone before the hoodie one comes round the corner of the house. He always picks it up. That is the entire point of him.

bobby built the box and the box works — i have watched it work, at length, from behind the amp. the trouble is that a box is only half of a prank. somebody has to carry it down the path and put it on the mat, and somebody has to not be standing there when he looks up. that somebody is me, because it was my idea to find out whether the hoodie one would take it off the grass. he always takes it off the grass. hold left or right to walk, set it down, and pick it up again if you are off the mat. the box is the easy half. — miki, from the doorway, holding the box

the takes and how long he leaves her

She runs it the same way he tests it, which is his fault: one clean getaway and the machine takes the next one harder. Every take shortens the window between pressing the button and the hoodie one coming round the corner, and tightens what counts as on the mat. He does not stop coming round the corner.

how it works plainly

Press the button and the box is in her arms. Hold left and right to walk, and set it down when she is on the mat — the glowing strip is the mat, and the wider the strip the earlier the take. Then walk her back past the door before the corner clock runs out. If she is still out there when he looks up, he sees her, waves, and takes the box anyway, which is the ending she likes best.

Set it down off the mat and he will still take it — he is not fussy, and the report will say so. You can pick it up again and have another go, but turning around costs seconds and seconds are the whole game. Then she prints the field report: the plant time, whether he saw her, how far clear she got, and what she thought of it.

the rule still the only rule

do not open the box.
