miki’s note, taped to the lid

found it in the back of the machines with the crank half turned, so obviously i finished the winding and put bobby’s head on it. it plays whatever you have the patience to turn: take 10 is sylvestre, take 11 is slay, and take 001 is bobby’s own — the only one on the box with a voice on it. he cut it himself and will not tell me how. put him back in and he swaps to the next one. the handle only goes one way. that is not a fault, that is the joke.

how to work it

grab the box and turn it. the handle is on the right, but the whole box will do — he does not care how the winding gets done, only that it gets done. six turns and the lid gives up. after that, the button. he hands you his menu too — read his menu — and it opens up on his left. tap a thing and he will tell you about it. add it to the order, press place it, and a slip comes out of the slot on the box with your order on it. he keeps a copy. the copies are on the spike. if it all gets away from you, reset the box puts him back in and starts the handle over.
