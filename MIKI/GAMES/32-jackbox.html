the order box · bobby, on a spring

Wind it up and bobby starts taking orders

A vintage order box, found in the back of the machines with the handle already half turned. Bobby's head is on it. Turn the crank and the box plays one of the twins' takes from the desk; keep turning and the lid gives up entirely. Then push the intercom. He has opinions about the special, and a menu he wrote himself.

miki’s note, taped to the lid

found it in the back of the machines with the crank half turned, so obviously i finished the winding and put bobby’s head on it. it plays whatever you have the patience to turn: take 10 is sylvestre, take 11 is slay. put him back in and he swaps. the handle only goes one way. that is not a fault, that is the joke.

how to work it

grab the box and turn it. the handle is on the right, but the whole box will do — he does not care how the winding gets done, only that it gets done. six turns and the lid gives up. after that, the button. ask him what’s on and he will read you the board — all nine of it, from memory, every time, in the same order he thinks it is in.
