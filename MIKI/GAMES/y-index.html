downstairs, past the machines she keeps

The room of games is mostly a room of machines

Miki keeps the machines, and under her place she keeps the ones that are only for playing on. Some are finished. Most are not. She says a room like this is never finished, it just gets one more working thing in it.

the first one’s done. bobby’s been sitting in it since tuesday, and he has not gotten tired of it once. wind him up, push the intercom, he’ll get your order started. be nice to him, he’s doing his best, and his best is very loud. and the claw’s plugged in now — it takes a jelly a go, so wind the box first, it pays. the counter beside the claw changes saved-up jellies into real things, which is the part i’m actually proud of. — miki, on the back of the lid

01 working

Bobby in the box

The order box, with Bobby’s head on it. Turn the crank and it plays one of the twins’ takes from the desk; keep turning and the lid gives up entirely. Then push the intercom and he takes your order. He has a menu he wrote himself — nine things, and not one of them is food — and a printer that hands you a slip with your order on it. He keeps a copy of every slip. The copies are on the spike.

02 working

the claw machine

The claw is very slow and very sincere. It has never once let go of anything on purpose, but it has also never once picked anything up on purpose, so it comes out even. It takes a jelly a go — the box pays jellies out — and there is a counter beside it that changes saved jellies into something real. There is also a corner of paper under the marbles. Miki says it’s nothing.
