editorial

the house,
in print —

“a zine is just a joke you keep laughing at, in print.”

— miki · editor-in-chief

the editor · yellow streak, lab-issued, kept on purpose

miki is half cat, half machine, all kindness — and the café's quiet security, watching over her siblings so she can brighten their days with silly pranks. never cruel, never revenge: a prank is only worth it if everyone ends up laughing, and she is quick to say a real sorry on the other side. the lab labeled her with a yellow streak, and she kept it because she earned it. she wrote this zine, the puns, and half the pranks in the building. page two didn't want to be written. page two lost. this page is the receipt.

09

machine-readable heart

miki's little
operating systems

the zine's terminal runs on miki os v2.7, loaded from a floppy of pure spite. do not lick the disk. the tea is on the left, the sarcasm is on the right — take both. ask her for a knock-knock joke and watch the web shiver.

her field guide to pranking a sibling, ethically: (1) make it reversible, (2) make it funny to everyone, (3) say sorry the moment you overstep, and mean it. she has practice. she's very quick.

$ ./siblings.sh --status
star: brewing ... hans: alphabetizing ... chari: question pending ... milo: gardening ... pearl: posing ... charlotte: mending ... wren: remembering ... all systems warm.

floppy: ready · puns: loaded

classifieds & the fine print

free to a good home
— one (1) slightly guilty conscience

wanted: a quiet corner to leave a thought you no longer need. payment in confessions and stray lines of poetry — we are not strict about the price. lost: one (1) sock, found in the poetry section. accusations pending. for sale: a slightly used catwalk, barely measured. pearl will model it personally.

the fine print: the light here is always on. wednesdays are especially good for arriving. if you smell violets, you are almost there — stand very still, and the web will carry you the rest of the way.
