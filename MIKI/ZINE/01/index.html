p. 02editorial

why the web is a safe place (and why i’m right)

welcome to issue 01. you are holding the thoughts of a chimera who refuses to be somebody’s footnote — which is me. i own this zine, the puns, and half the pranks in this building. the other half are chari’s. she’s learning.

the web here is safe because the light is always on. that’s the whole argument. it’s not a metaphor, check the ceiling.

inside this issue: an interview with me (i interviewed myself, who else would do it right), an ad for a model who has already decided violet is this season, a spider who mends, a free song you can listen to with your phone, recipes from the tea committee, and a field guide to the fourth shelf. it bites. it’s a joke. it doesn’t bite. probably.

— miki, who is watching. affectionately.

p. 08the ritual

knock knock, ethically

the knock knock is a ritual, not a suggestion. here is the sanctioned method, field-tested on siblings.

you: knock knock.
sibling: who’s there?
you: [a word].
sibling: [word] who?
you: the punchline. deliver it deadpan. do not smile. smiling is forfeit.

afterward, accept the reaction with dignity. the sibling may throw a cushion. this is part of the ritual.

how to prank your sibling, ethically

1. pranks must be reversible. ctrl+z exists. use it.

2. nobody gets wet, cold, hungry, or late. nobody’s tea is confiscated.

3. if the target laughs within the hour, you win. if they laugh and then plan revenge, you win more.

4. the spider must not be poked. the web mends enough holes already.

5. when caught, confess in writing. the zine prints confessions on page 14, under “evidence.”

recipes from the café

star’s three-sugar tea (the official beverage of the violet web)

ingredients: one (1) pot, tea of choice, three sugars, zero questions, the void asks for none.

method: brew the tea properly, as if it matters, because it does. add exactly three sugars — no more, no less, no negotiation. pour with intent. serve with the saucer (the saucer is not a hat).

star says the tea is ready and she means it this time.

p. 14field guide

field guide: the fourth shelf

the fourth shelf of the archive is a recurring source of local legend. this guide clarifies everything.

fact: the fourth shelf bites. fact: it’s a joke. fact: it doesn’t bite. probably.

proper conduct when the shelf has been bitten (or not):

1. do not apologize to the shelf. it is a shelf. (if you must, apologize to the book you startled.)

2. report the bite to hans. he will alphabetize the report.

3. if the shelf bites and then apologizes, that is not the shelf. that is chari, who is learning.

4. never reorganize the fourth shelf. last sibling to try is still finding their pen.

it’s a joke.
probably.
