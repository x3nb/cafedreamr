the catalogue is the whole shelf in order — one card per issue, nothing hidden behind a mosaic. every issue is a web page and every issue prints the same way: open it, press print, fold it down the middle, staple twice. issue no. 1 also exists in two editions: the violet café flip book and the hologram edition. the back issues are at /ZINE/01/, /ZINE/02/ and /ZINE/03/; no. 4 is with the printer.

the press

how you actually get one · all three ways are free
01 take one at the counter

miki leaves a stack by the register, hand-numbered and stapled, and refills it when she remembers. if the stack is empty, ask — she usually has more in the library.

02 print it at home

every issue is a web page, built to print. open the issue, hit the print button, fold it down the middle, staple it twice. that is the whole production budget.

03 make the machine cough one up

the vendi machine always keeps one zine in the glass, no matter what else it's been asked for. slot it and it hands you a copy for nothing.

the honest bit

the zine is free.
the press isn't.

fifty copies is the smallest run the printer will do, and miki pays for the whole thing up front — the paper, the cover stock, the binding, the staples — and then gives every single one away. nobody has ever been charged for an issue and nobody ever will be.

so if you want to keep the rack full: the same fund that holds the realm open pays the printer. a coffee on ko-fi buys ink and paper. a commission from the menu pays for a piece and puts your name in the back of the next issue.
