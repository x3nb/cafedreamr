      what this is: a proof sheet, not the zine. every word on the cover is editable in the COVER block at the bottom of this file — the headline, the three cover lines, the contents, the issue number and the run size — so the front and the back can never disagree. the twins are drawn, not photographed: two inked plates (../images/slay-sylvestre-a.jpg and -b.jpg, one shelf up), close-up and back-to-back, and the two-frame button cuts between them — that is the animation. slay & sylvestre are identical twins, and they are kids — high school, clean-shaven, no beards and no age on them at all — drawn with snow-white hair and blue eyes, clean faces with nothing painted on them — one design that carries both the lore plates and the miki tv plates — and the blue of those eyes is the only colour on the whole sheet. hands are outside the frame on purpose, which is the joke on fig. 01.
