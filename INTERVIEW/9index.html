meet your hosts

five mentors, twelve questions between them. they ask in this order: chari, then milo, then star, then hans, then miki. read who teaches what — then sit. tap a card to enlarge.

chari

keeper of the atrium · realm 02

everyone’s apprentice, freshly upgraded — she owns her own castle now. two white rats, crumb and mochi, ride her shoulders. she teaches care — how to sit with people and mean it. one day she may counsel like pearl, or open a therapy pet café.

milo

keeper of the garden · realm 04

mint stripes. the still one. one pot of tea, a hammock, and cushions nobody has been asked to move. he teaches meditation and prayer. hand him the heavy thing you’re carrying and he will leave it by the wall — and it will not follow you out.

star

host of cafedreamr · keeps the keys

she runs the counter and most of the doors. she teaches brewing a real cup of coffee and holding one imagined thing through any noise — squirrels come along daily and she always finds her way back to the cup. her cat, char, greets everybody who comes in.

hans

steward of the light-kingdom · realm 01

white wavy hair to his shoulders with black streaks, foxbat-human and vampire-redeemed. old royal manners, tea etiquette, earl grey on time — God first, then Yeshua, then tea. there is a proper way to do almost everything, and he knows it. he teaches study, ceremony, and etiquette from his archives library. he jumps portals and bites no one — by agreement with Yeshua he kept his vampire teleportation, and arrives wherever he feels like it.

miki

keeper of the games room · realm 03

mole-rat energy and the loudest quiet-breaker in the building. the zine, the machines, and hans's tidy order box — which has her sock in it. she teaches loud making — zines, machines, band practice — and watching the dark portals with slay and sylvestre so nothing weird comes through. she lives in a cave. practice is starting and the door's propped — no kettle though: she doesn't do tea.
