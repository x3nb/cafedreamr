our mission

leave lighter
than you arrived

a place to unload heavy thoughts

Every part of this realm — the counter, the rooms, the music, the small glowing machines — is built for exactly one reason. That everyone who plays here should feel lighter when they leave than when they walked in. Not distracted. Not entertained until they forget. Lighter: the way you feel when you finally set something heavy down, and someone kind takes it from you and says i’ve got it from here.

That is the whole mission. Everything else on this site is in service to it.

01 · the intention

why we’re allowed to promise that

we didn’t invent this idea — we’re following it

We want to be clear about where the idea came from, because it isn’t ours.

Yeshua — Jesus Christ — said plainly what he wanted for everyone on earth: to feel light, and not to carry the burdens of daily life around in their heart. To feel joy and not dissatisfaction. Not on a good day. Every day. For everybody — no exceptions, no entrance requirements, nobody standing at the door being measured first.

So when this realm says come in and put it down, we aren’t being poetic. We’re repeating him. The cafe is a small, warm room built in the shape of that promise — and if it ever stops feeling like that, then something here has gone wrong and we want you to tell us.

Everything you find in this place is aimed at that: the music you can leave running, the rooms you can wander without being asked a single question, the characters who are simply glad you came. You are never required to perform, to be interesting, to be fine.

02 · what the chimeras are saying

nobody here is a defect

the hybrid animals, the ears, the tails — and the truth they stand in for

In this story there are chimeras: hybrid animals who were made into humans — some with animal ears, some with tails, some with both. The world that made them called them experiments. It looked at them, measured them against something, and quietly decided they had come out wrong.

They are not decoration. They are the argument.

Because this is what the chimeras are standing in for, and it is true for every single person reading this page:

  • No human being on earth is perfect. Not one. The polished ones, the calm ones, the ones who look like they got it right — they have their own trials, and they carry them the same way you carry yours.
  • Everyone goes through trials. It does not matter what any of us has been through, how heavy the year was, or how much of it we are still carrying around. Nobody is exempt, and nobody is being graded on how well they hid it.
  • No matter what we look like, and no matter what we feel like — on a good morning or a bad one — it does not change the way Yeshua sees us. Not by a single degree. Man looks at the outward appearance; the Lord looks at the heart.

The world will spend your whole life trying to convince you that you are defective — that something is missing, that you were assembled wrong, that you are a copy of a copy of a copy. That voice is loud and it is everywhere and it is wrong.

Yeshua says: you are my jewel. Not a stone among stones. Not a spare part. Precious diamonds — worth more to him than anything ever dug out of the ground, and he says it the way a person speaks about something they would never, ever sell.

And then comes the part that changes everything.

When he dwells inside a person, he makes them new. Not improved. Not patched. Not tolerated while they work on themselves. New. If there was darkness in you, it is not there any more — it is finished, it is gone, it is not the thing he is looking at when he looks at you. God does not see a dim little creature who still needs a lot of work. He sees his own precious light.

Like the lightning bug. A tiny, ordinary creature that carries its own lamp — and goes on glowing. In a field, on a warm night, when everything around it has gone grey, it does not argue with the dark. It just glows, because that is what it was made to do.

That is you. That is the whole design.

This is the firefly that follows your cursor around the realm. It is here for a reason: it is the smallest, plainest picture we have of what Yeshua does with a person who lets him in. You do not have to be big. You do not have to be bright on your own. You only have to be his — and then the light is not something you have to manufacture, because it is living in you.

03 · no longer liable

the account is closed

not suspended · not on probation · closed

We are not guilty.

Jesus Christ died in our place. Not as an example, not as a gesture, not as a general statement about being nicer to each other — in our place, the way one person stands in for another when the fine has to be paid and the one who owes it cannot possibly pay it. He paid it. It is paid.

And when we are baptised, we surrender our old life to God, so that Yeshua can take our place. Buried with him; raised with him; walking in newness of life. The old account is not suspended and we are not on probation pending good behaviour. The old account is closed, and the new one is not ours to keep in order — it is his.

So: we are no longer liable. No longer judged. Free in Yeshua — free the way a person is free who has walked out of a courtroom with nothing left owing, and no file with their name on it.

Now we live in him, and in his kingdom — which he shares with utter love and devotion to those who love him. Not as servants kept at a polite distance in the hallway. As family at the table, with a plate already set out.

04 · what he left behind

he handed us his authority

so speak into the air — he is listening

And when he left the earth, he did not leave us empty-handed. He left us his authority. We inherited it. That is not a metaphor and it is not a small thing — it is the reason a person who has nothing can still stand up and change the shape of their day.

So now we speak things into the air, and he hears us. He creates the things we ask for — or he helps us, quietly, in ways we only notice afterwards — and where there was a wall there is suddenly a new door of opportunity waiting, already open, that we would have sworn was not there last week.

He makes all things new. Nothing is impossible for us with Jesus Christ leading the way. Not because we are impressive — because he is, and he has decided to take us with him.

05 · the honest part

what it costs to keep the light on

no markup · no invented numbers · here is the actual bill

The realm is small enough right now to run almost free. That is the honest truth, and we are not going to dress it up to make the ask look bigger than it is.

But we don’t want it to stay small. We want these rooms to hold as many members and visitors as possible — a whole weekend’s worth of people, all arriving at once, nobody turned away — without the doors jamming, without the music cutting out, and without anyone losing what they were in the middle of.

We want this to be your favourite place to chill out and lounge: somewhere you can learn cool new things about yourself, and about how to respect and love each other. It turns out that is a modest dream with a very un-modest electricity bill. So here it is, line by line.

That is the full, unfiltered number — the roof, the electricity and the people, all at once. It is a lot, and we would rather show it to you than quietly round it off.

this month’s goal is not the whole number. The next step is the roof and the electricity plus one community host — enough that the realm stays up through a busy weekend and that a real person is there to answer you. That lands at about $1,500 a month, and that is what the ko-fi goal is set to.

how the money arrives: ko-fi takes a 5% service fee on shop sales and commissions; card processors take about 2.9% + 30¢ a transaction, and a card issued outside the US adds about 1.5%. We do not add a cent on top of our own costs to cover it — the fees simply come out of the gift, which is why a small gift doesn’t stretch quite as far as it looks.

what we promise: no markup on anything we make, and no number on this page that we invented to look good. Where a figure is an estimate (a busy weekend is a guess, and a friendly one) we say so. When a real bill comes in and differs, we change the number on this page and tell you why.

and what we don’t do: no paywalls, no locked rooms, no “premium” version of feeling better. The realm is free and will stay free. Nobody’s ability to be here, or to be cared about, will ever depend on whether they could give anything.
