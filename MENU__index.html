    01 · tell me

    message me with the piece you want, or just the feeling you're after. a screenshot, a playlist, one word — all fine.

    02 · i quote it

    i reply with an itemised cost sheet — my hours, the materials, the fees and the post — usually within a couple of days. nothing is paid until we've agreed.

    03 · i sketch

    i share the early stages — for a garment, the toile, the first rough version in cheap cloth — so we're both sure it's heading where you want it before it goes further.

    finished, sent to you, and your name goes in the back of the zine. the piece stays your story.
